Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a project?

Contact Design Castle with your room type, preferred style, location and approximate budget. The team can discuss your requirements and next steps.

Can I request a particular interior style?

Yes. Share your preferred colours, reference images, materials and the atmosphere you want to create.

How much will my interior project cost?

Costs depend on the space, materials, furniture, finishes and scope of work. The website calculator is only an illustrative planning tool, not a quotation.

Can I contact you through Instagram?

Yes. Visit the official Design Castle Instagram profile using the link on this website.

Are the gallery pictures completed Design Castle projects?

The current gallery uses illustrative interior inspiration images. Replace these with verified company project photographs when available.
